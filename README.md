## What is an AI visibility tool?

An AI visibility tool asks AI engines the questions your buyers ask and records whether your brand is mentioned, where it appears in the answer, how it is described and which competitors are named instead. It is the AI search counterpart to an SEO rank tracker. The practice of improving the result is called Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) or Answer Engine Optimization (AEO).

Two kinds of tool exist. A **checker** gives a one off snapshot. A **platform** or **tracker** reruns the same questions on a schedule, so you can see the trend, benchmark competitors and tell whether your changes are working.

## How to choose

1. **Engine coverage.** Your buyers use more than one engine. Check which engines are in the plan you would buy, not only the top tier.
2. **Buyer relevant prompts.** A tool is only as good as the questions it asks. Twenty real buying questions beat two hundred generic ones.
3. **A score you understand.** Every vendor calculates its score differently, so scores are not comparable between tools. Judge the trend within one tool.
4. **Regular refresh.** AI answers change between runs, so ask how often prompts are rerun and whether you can read the full answers.
5. **Competitor benchmarking.** You need to see who AI names instead of you, question by question.
6. **A plan, not only a dashboard.** Look for a prioritised list of what to fix first.

Start with two free checkers that cover different engines, then move to a platform once you have found a gap worth closing.
